## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🛡️ [TRACE](https://github.com/niviya-albert/TRACE): AI Reunification & Claim Verification Engine
*MakersNeedMore Hackathon: selected for Round 2*
Lost-and-found matching that fuses **CLIP image embeddings, MiniLM text embeddings, location and time** into one confidence score, with a plain-language reason for every match and a **fraud-resistant claim check** that never leaks the hidden detail.
`Python` `PyTorch` `FastAPI` `SQLite` `Streamlit` `Pytest`

### 🧭 [SAHAY](https://github.com/niviya-albert/sahay): Citizen Case Navigator for Kerala Government Services · [Live Demo](https://9c9k63mu9ugvuhmh5kuwgn.streamlit.app/)
*OOSC 4.0 Hackathon: Round 1 prototype*
A sourced service graph plus a rule-based diagnosis engine. **The LLM only interprets; deadlines and required documents are computed by deterministic code**, so it can't invent a government fact.
`Python` `Streamlit` `Gemini API`

### 📉 [AI SaaS Growth Intelligence System](https://github.com/niviya-albert/saas-growth-intelligence)
Churn prediction and 0–100 risk scoring on 7,032 customer records (**76.74% recall**), with cohort and survival analysis, revenue-at-risk metrics (MRR, ARR, LTV:CAC, NRR), a six-page Streamlit app and Gemini-generated executive briefings. CI with GitHub Actions.
`Python` `SQL` `Scikit-learn` `Streamlit` `Gemini API`

### 🚗 [AutoValue AI](https://github.com/niviya-albert/AutoValue-AI): Used-Car Price Prediction
Four regression models compared with cross-validation on a leakage-free pipeline. **Best: Gradient Boosting, 0.848 R²**, deployed as a Streamlit valuation app.
`Python` `Scikit-learn` `Streamlit`

### 📊 [WorkPulse](https://github.com/niviya-albert/WorkPulse): India Unemployment & COVID-19 Impact
Quantifies the lockdown shock (9.51% pre-COVID average to a 24.88% peak) and the rural-urban gap, with an interactive dashboard.
`Python` `Pandas` `Seaborn` `Plotly` `Streamlit`

- **Tested and reproducible:** pytest suites, CI, and one-command setup
